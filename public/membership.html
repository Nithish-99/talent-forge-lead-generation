PROJECT PARTICIPATION

Understand the work model.

Project work is governed by the programme rules and the individual project agreement.

01

Project consideration

Talent Forge describes a monthly project programme. Matching is based on membership level, verified professional skills, portfolio, availability, performance and suitable project requirements.

02

Project acceptance

Review the project scope, deliverables, quality requirements, deadline, communication expectations and terms before accepting the assignment.

03

Completion and review

Complete the agreed deliverables and applicable review process. Rejection or incomplete work may affect eligibility for the relevant membership month, according to programme terms.

04

Applicable compensation

Eligible project-based compensation is processed after work completion and approval, according to the individual project agreement. It is not a salary promise.

READ BEFORE ENROLLING

Important membership conditions.

Membership is a programme, not a job offer. Read the current official membership agreement and project terms before making a payment or accepting work.

Membership fee: One-time programme fee for the selected membership period, as published by the official website.

Eligibility: Membership level, skill verification, portfolio and availability can affect project consideration.

Project work: Each assignment is governed by its scope, deliverables, deadline, quality and communication requirements.

Compensation: Project-based and subject to completion, approval and the applicable agreement. Not salary or guaranteed income.

Other terms: Refund, cancellation, intellectual property, confidentiality and disputes are governed by the official membership agreement and project-specific terms.

MEMBERSHIP FAQs

Before you enrol.

These answers are a guide. The current official membership agreement and project-specific terms take priority.

Does membership guarantee employment?

No. Talent Forge membership is not employment and does not promise a job, salary or fixed monthly income.

Is a project assignment automatic?

Project consideration is subject to the applicable membership terms, verified skills, availability, suitability, performance and project requirements. Review the official programme wording carefully.

Are the displayed project values guaranteed?

No. They are illustrative ranges published by the official website. Actual project value and compensation depend on the project scope, deliverables, approval and individual project agreement.

What happens if I reject or do not complete an assigned project?

The official page describes consequences for rejected or incomplete assignments. Check the current programme and project terms before accepting a project.

Where do I complete payment and enrolment?

Use only the official Alpha Auron Talent Forge website and its approved enrolment and payment process.

Where can I check refunds, cancellation and dispute terms?

Refer to the current membership agreement and the specific project agreement. Ask Alpha Auron for clarification before paying or accepting work.
