FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Review the basics before sending your membership enquiry.

What is Talent Forge?

Talent Forge is a professional membership and project network operated by Alpha Auron Private Limited. Eligible members can develop their professional profile and be considered for selected projects under the applicable terms.

Who can join Talent Forge?

Students, graduates, freelancers and working professionals may enquire. Membership eligibility, skill verification and project readiness are determined under the official programme requirements.

What membership plans are available?

The official page currently lists Starter, Professional, Specialist, Elite and Auron levels with 6-month and 12-month options. Fees and terms can change, so confirm them on the official website.

How much does membership cost?

Membership fees vary by level and duration. The plan cards above show the amounts retrieved from the public official page on 7 November 2026. Confirm the current fee before payment.

How does project allocation work?

Project consideration and allocation depend on the membership level, verified skills, portfolio, availability, performance, suitability and current project requirements. Refer to the official membership agreement for the controlling conditions.

Will every member receive a monthly project?

The official Talent Forge page describes a monthly project programme, subject to the conditions stated there. A project assignment is not a job, salary or guaranteed fixed income. Read the official programme terms carefully.

How does project compensation work?

Compensation is project-based. Applicable payout depends on the agreed scope, deliverables, completion, approval and project-specific agreement. Published project values are illustrative, not guaranteed earnings.

When are payouts processed?

Payout timing is governed by the applicable project agreement and approval process. The public page does not establish one universal payout date for every project.

How do I complete enrolment?

Submit an enquiry first. The Talent Forge representative can guide you to the detailed membership information and official enrolment process. Enquiry submission itself is not membership enrolment.

Can I contact a Talent Forge Associate?

Yes. Use the verified contact information shown in the employee contact section below, when provided by the company.
